# Potion Panic

A playable Unity 6 potion-shop game based on the supplied proposal. Artwork and short sound effects are generated by the game; no asset downloads are required.

## Play

**Windows:** launch `Builds/Windows/Potion Panic.exe`. Keep the executable beside its data folder and supporting files.

Open the project in Unity **6000.3.10f1**, open `Assets/Scenes/SampleScene.unity`, and press **Play**. The interface starts automatically. Choose **Learn your first potion** for guided practice, or **Open the shop** for a five-customer day.

- Click customers to take orders; click persistent tickets to switch potions.
- Select ingredients and **hold** Pour to match amounts. Empty mixture lets you retry before committing.
- Start brewing, then stop in the **8–10 second** green zone. Brewing continues at other stations.
- Hold Stir to **70**, release, and click Finish. Stirring occupies a cauldron until finished.
- Choose the requested bottle and garnish, select the matching numbered ticket, and serve.
- **Escape** pauses; losing focus also pauses. Pause includes restart and main menu.

The shop has three recipes, three ingredients, two bottles, two garnishes, three active-ticket spaces, and two cauldrons. Customers arrive every 22 seconds. Serve all five to finish the day. Ingredients are unlimited and mistakes are recoverable.

## Scoring

Ingredient accuracy, brewing/stirring, finishing, and patience are averaged equally. Heat timing is perfect at 8–10 seconds; unattended brews turn off at 15 seconds with a penalty. Stirring is best at 70. Patience starts declining after 70 seconds. Base payment is 5–20 coins, plus up to 5 in tips. The results ledger shows each customer's four ratings. Practice has no patience penalty.

## Build and development

Use **Potion Panic → Build Windows game** in Unity to produce `Builds/Windows/Potion Panic.exe`. Keep its accompanying files together when sharing it. Existing scene assets are preserved.

- `Assets/PotionPanic/PotionGame.cs`: independent order state and scoring.
- `Assets/PotionPanic/PotionPanicApp.cs`: interface, procedural artwork, sound, and game flow.
- `Assets/PotionPanic/Editor/PotionPanicBuild.cs`: Windows build command.
- `Tests/GameRulesChecks.cs`: executable rule checks covering concurrent brews, capacity, wrong tickets, overheating, and full-day completion.

Run `./Tests/Run-Checks.ps1` in PowerShell after Unity has imported the project to repeat the gameplay checks and runtime-code compilation check.

For rendered smoke checks, launch the built executable with `-potion-capture "C:/absolute/output/folder"`. This optional check captures seven screens, completes a perfect practice order, writes `complete.txt`, and exits. It is inactive during normal play.

The interface scales to the window with letterboxing. This first version uses procedural 2D artwork. Progress lasts for the current session; upgrades, extra days, and saved progression are outside its scope.
